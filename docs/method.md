# Method and Definitions

## Productivity growth

```text
real_resources     = cash expenditure / (deflator_index / 100)
resource_growth    = real_resources_t / real_resources_(t-1) - 1
activity_growth    = weighted_activity_t / weighted_activity_(t-1) - 1
productivity_growth = (1 + activity_growth) / (1 + resource_growth) - 1
```

## Known limits

- Productivity is a ratio. Pay awards raise resources without raising treatment, which lowers measured productivity mechanically.
- Activity recorded by one trust may be generated by staff employed by another.
- Association is not causation.
